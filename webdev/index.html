MAURYAN EMPIRE:

The empire was set up in 324 BC by a great king Chandragupta Maurya who defeated Dhana Nandathe ruler of Nanda dynasty.

There are three rulers in Mauryan empire.They are:

Chandragupta Maurya

Bindusara

Ashoka

There were many rulers after Ashoka but they were weak. So they managed to rule very hardly and have lost many territories won by The Great Ashoka
